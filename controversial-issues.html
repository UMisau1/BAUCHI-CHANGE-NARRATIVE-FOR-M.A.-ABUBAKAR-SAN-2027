MOHAMMED ABDULLAHI ABUBAKAR, SAN

Bauchi State 2015–2019

Introduction

This section presents selected issues, records, documents and public information relating to the administration of His Excellency Mohammed Abdullahi Abubakar, SAN, as Governor of Bauchi State from 31 May 2015 to 31 May 2019.

The purpose of this section is to provide visitors with access to relevant documentary records and publicly available information so that the issues can be examined from the available evidence.
