OUR MANIFESTO

Our Vision for a Greater Bauchi State

A practical roadmap for sustainable development, prosperity, accountability and inclusive governance.

Quality Education

Improve access to quality education through school renovation, teacher development, ICT integration and scholarship programmes.

Healthcare

Strengthen primary healthcare centres, improve maternal healthcare, provide modern medical equipment and ensure affordable healthcare services.

Agriculture

Support farmers with improved seedlings, fertilizers, irrigation facilities, extension services and better access to markets.

Youth Empowerment

Create employment opportunities, vocational training, entrepreneurship support and digital skills programmes for young people.

Women Development

Promote women's participation in governance, improve access to finance and expand empowerment programmes for women across the state.

Economic Growth

Encourage investment, promote SMEs, improve infrastructure and create a business-friendly environment for sustainable economic development.
